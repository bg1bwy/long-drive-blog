### Preserving Data Beyond Platform Lifecycles
Traditional cloud storage often relies on subscription models that can expire, leading to file deletion or restricted access. For businesses managing compliance archives, professionals safeguarding intellectual property, or individuals preserving years of photographs and videos, relying on transient servers introduces unnecessary risk. Permanent storage addresses this by decoupling data retention from ongoing payments. Once uploaded and paid for, the information is cryptographically anchored across a decentralized network, ensuring it remains accessible indefinitely without recurring fees.
