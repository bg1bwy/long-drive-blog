According to a recent market analysis by Global Market Insights Inc., the global decentralized storage sector is projected to exceed USD 4.5 billion by 2034. This steady growth underscores a fundamental shift in how organizations approach data infrastructure, moving away from centralized silos toward more resilient, distributed networks.

As digital archives continue to expand, maintaining long-term accessibility has become a complex challenge. Traditional cloud providers often rely on recurring subscription models that can inadvertently lead to data loss if payments lapse or services are discontinued. This reality has accelerated the adoption of permanent storage architectures. By utilizing blockchain-based consensus mechanisms and prepaid storage fees, these systems guarantee that uploaded files remain accessible indefinitely without continuous vendor intervention.
