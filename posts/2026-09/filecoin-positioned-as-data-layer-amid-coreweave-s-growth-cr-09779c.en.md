### Beyond Temporary Cloud Storage
Traditional cloud providers operate on subscription models that can change pricing or service terms without notice. Permanent storage addresses this by shifting the model from rent-to-own to a one-time commitment, ensuring files remain accessible without recurring fees or service interruptions.
