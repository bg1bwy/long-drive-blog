On August 28, automotive service chain Yellow Hat disclosed a potential unauthorized access incident affecting up to 1,801,499 users of its web appointment system. While credit card details were reportedly safe, names and phone numbers remain exposed. This latest incident serves as a stark reminder that traditional centralized cloud storage is vulnerable to both cyberattacks and long-term service discontinuation. When sensitive corporate archives or personal memories are stored on conventional platforms, data loss remains a persistent risk over time due to server migrations, policy changes, or platform shutdowns. Organizations must adopt more resilient archival strategies to protect critical information.

To address these vulnerabilities, forward-thinking teams are turning to permanent storage solutions built on decentralized networks.
