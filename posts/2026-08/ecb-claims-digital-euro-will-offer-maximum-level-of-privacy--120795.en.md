### The Shift Toward Long-Term Data Preservation
Financial records are just one category at risk. Historical records and personal memories degrade quickly when tied to centralized servers that can change terms overnight or cease operations without warning. True data sovereignty requires an infrastructure where you control both the location and access keys, independent of any single company’s business model.
