As digital assets and sensitive information become increasingly valuable, protecting them extends beyond financial metrics to how we securely archive and retain data over time. The demand for privacy naturally evolves into a demand for reliability, especially when individuals and organizations consider what will happen to their digital footprint in ten or twenty years.

Traditional cloud storage often introduces friction through tiered pricing, sudden platform sunsets, or opaque data handling policies. When the goal is long-term retention, users need infrastructure that prioritizes immutability and user ownership over short-term monetization. This is where permanent data preservation becomes a practical necessity rather than a niche feature. Systems must guarantee that files are not only encrypted but also anchored to a network designed for infinite durability, removing the administrative burden of renewal reminders.
